Record switch

Recording behaviour

Record on arm

Starts recording automatically when Betaflight arms.

Stop on disarm

Stop the recording when the FC disarms. If off, keep recording until the switch goes low or you stop it here.

Grace period after disarm before the camera actually stops, handy if you disarm to trigger Crash Flip and might re-arm again. Re-arming within this window cancels the stop.
The record switch always overrides: flipping it OFF clears an armed-start. Camera commands are absolute start/stop, never toggles, so reconnect retries are safe.

Wi-Fi access point

Wi-Fi access point enabled

Master switch. Off = the Wi-Fi radio never starts, not even at boot, and the switch below can't bring it back. Use it when you don't want any Wi-Fi next to your ELRS receiver.

Turning this off disconnects this page. To turn Wi-Fi back on later, use the FPVShutter Configurator over USB or Betaflight serial passthrough.
Assign a spare switch: flip it low in flight and the Wi-Fi radio powers down completely (saves battery, BLE camera link keeps running). Flip high to bring the hotspot back. While the access point is enabled it always starts ON at boot, so the switch can't lock you out.
Saving restarts the access point and your phone will disconnect. Reconnect to the new network name to continue.

Bluetooth power

Sets how strongly the camera link transmits. High gives the most range and is the default. Try Medium or Low if your ELRS receiver loses range when this board sits right next to it; keep the camera close if you do. Takes effect straight away, with no reconnect.

Discover new camera

Pick your camera model, then scan. The model decides how FPVShutter talks to the camera.

Scanning…
Camera not listed? Show all nearby devices
Showing every advertiser with a valid address. Hold the camera within 1 m, because the strongest signal is usually yours. Pick your device and tap Pair & Save; if the connection fails with “not a <brand> camera” you picked the wrong one.
How pairing works: 1) pick the model → 2) press Scan for Cameras → 3) power the camera on nearby → 4) tap Pair & Save on the device. Nothing connects until you confirm. First GoPro connection needs one approval tap on the camera screen; DJI cameras show an approve prompt the first time too.

Custom messages 1–4

Each slot feeds one of Betaflight's four Custom Message OSD elements over MSP (MSP2_SET_TEXT, max 16 chars). Build it from an optional label plus up to four elements; they're joined with spaces and anything that doesn't fit whole is dropped. The preview updates as you edit, before saving. Position the elements in the Betaflight Configurator OSD tab. Elements marked n/a aren't reported by the selected camera and will show --.

MSP console

Send a command to talk to your FC over MSP…
About configuring Betaflight from this page: this console is a real MSP passthrough: your browser talks to the FC through the ESP32, exactly like Betaflight Configurator does over serial. A full configurator port isn't realistic on an ESP32-C3 (it's a multi-megabyte app), but everything here is scriptable JavaScript: read commands are enabled via an allowlist today, and safe parameter writes can be added on top of the same endpoint later. Think "Lua scripts, in your browser".
